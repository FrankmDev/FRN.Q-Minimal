## Definition

frnq.es is the **Spanish commercial surface** of FRN.Q, the independent digital practice of Francisco Muñoz (Granada, Spain).

- **frnq.es** — commercial. Spanish. Sells outcomes: ecommerce, B2B commerce, systems, web & growth.
- **frnq.studio** — professional/technical portfolio. English. Evidence of craft and engineering. Linked **externally** as `STUDIO ↗` everywhere (header, hero, mobile menu, contact and footer), never merged or duplicated.

frnq.es is **not** a translation of frnq.studio, **not** an English portfolio with a language switch, and **not** an agency site. It is a one-person, named practice with direct execution.

## Audience

Decision-makers in small and medium businesses who need to sell, order or operate through digital systems:

- Owners and managers who sell online and need a store or catalogue that works.
- Businesses with professional customers (distribution, wholesale, B2B) who manage orders, private prices and accounts by hand.
- Teams whose data and processes live in disconnected tools.
- Small businesses, local businesses and independent professionals who need a trustworthy website, clear services and accessible contact, whether or not they sell online.
- Businesses with a website already in place that needs repair, recovery or extension rather than a full rebuild (WordPress, Elementor, WooCommerce).
- Granada businesses in tourism, accommodation, restaurants, bars, venues and retail that need a working digital presence, a direct sales channel and connected systems.

They buy **outcomes and reduced friction**, not technology. They care about their process, their customers and what they can measure.

## Commercial hierarchy (priority order)

1. **ECOMMERCE & SHOPIFY** — D2C selling: catalogue, product, checkout, measurement, connected operations.
2. **PORTALES & SISTEMAS B2B** — professional customers: private prices, orders, accounts, roles, portals.
3. **SISTEMAS & INTEGRACIONES** — integrations, workflows, connected data, internal tools. Automation is one capability inside this area, never a separate specialisation.
4. **WEB, SEO & GROWTH** (shown publicly as "Web corporativa & SEO") — corporate web, repair/recovery and extension of existing websites, technical/local SEO, analytics and conversion.

On the Home the four areas are ordered with **Web corporativa & SEO** first, because a professional website and the repair or improvement of an existing one are the fastest entry point for new work. This is a Home presentation order, not a change of depth: Ecommerce and B2B keep their full commercial weight, their dedicated routes and their evidence.

Design (UI/UX), frontend development, web, technical/local SEO, analytics, CRO, accessibility, performance and integrations are **capabilities** used across those areas. They are supporting means, never the headline or a separate top-level service. Do not promise every capability on every engagement.

## Value proposition

> FRN.Q helps a business sell better, order its processes and measure what matters through ecommerce, portals and systems built and directed by one person from discovery to launch.

Three durable promises, all defensible:

1. **Trato directo.** One interlocutor: the person who decides is the person who builds. No account layers, no hand-offs.
2. **Entender el negocio primero.** Every engagement starts from how the business actually works, not from a template or a technology preference.
3. **Sistemas que se pueden mantener.** Documented, ownable, evolvable. Not a black box the client cannot leave.

## Web Rescue (revisión y reparación web)

A productized, low-friction entry point for businesses that already have a website:

- **Diagnóstico Web 360 — 99 €.** An initial, scoped review: what fails, what can be improved and where to start. Discounted from an eligible repair hired within the next 30 days. It is **not** an unlimited audit.
- **Reparación de landing — desde 299 €.** Corrections and improvements on a single page.
- **Webs mayores — presupuesto.** Quoted after the review, according to incidents and complexity.

Process: **Diagnóstico → Presupuesto → Reparación → Verificación**. The scope and price are agreed in writing before any repair. This block is published on the Home page and its entry offers are the productized part of the repair service; the web starting points, Shopify, Care and the rescue offers are published together on `/precios/`, while B2B, systems, integrations and custom work are quoted after discovery. It is not a new specialisation and does not replace the four commercial areas.

## Soporte y mantenimiento (FRN.Q Support)

A continuity service for sites and systems FRN.Q has delivered, or can take on
after a review. It keeps what works running and handles planned changes. It is
**not** unlimited support and does not promise generic response times or 24/7
availability.

- **Mantenimiento técnico:** reviews, necessary updates and incidents inside the
  agreed scope.
- **Cambios menores y mejoras planificadas:** small changes, grouped and planned.
- **Revisión técnica y seguimiento:** performance, errors, backups and basic
  security at the agreed cadence.
- **Evolución, SEO, datos e integraciones:** new work, defined through a specific
  agreement, never folded into maintenance.

Scope, response times, availability, hosting, licences and extraordinary work are
agreed in writing. It is not a new commercial area. The public starting point is
the CARE price (from 49 €/mes, see `src/data/pricing.ts`), which `/soporte/`
reads rather than restating. Published at `/soporte/` with the CTA "Consultar
soporte" (contact type `soporte`).
